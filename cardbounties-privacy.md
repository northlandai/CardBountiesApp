# Card Bounties Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 3, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

Card Bounties is operated by Gary Gallagher in Minnesota under the Card
Bounties and Northland AI brands (collectively, “Card Bounties,” “we,” “us,” or
“our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and
protect personal information when you use the Card Bounties iOS application,
our related websites and support channels, and our Verification Partner
program (collectively, the “Service”).

Contact us at **support@northlandai.com** with privacy questions or requests.

## 1. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Service.

### Account and contact information

We may collect your name, email address, telephone number, account identifier,
handle, display name, profile image, city, state or region, country, account
status, consent records, and authentication events. If you use Sign in with
Apple, Apple may provide an account identifier and, depending on your choices,
your name and an email address or private relay address.

### Identity, eligibility, and fraud-prevention information

Before a user may place a binding bounty, claim a card, receive a payout, or
complete certain high-value activity, we or our providers may collect or verify
government identification, a selfie, age, address, tax details, payment or bank
readiness, proof of funds, source-of-funds information, sanctions or fraud
signals, and related review results.

Stripe collects and stores the identity document images and sensitive payment
credentials used in its hosted flows. Card Bounties receives verification
status, provider session identifiers, limited verified attributes when needed,
and an irreversible keyed identity fingerprint used to help detect duplicate
identities. We do not intend to store raw payment-card numbers or raw identity
document images in Card Bounties databases.

### Payment and transaction information

We may collect payment-provider customer and connected-account identifiers,
payment-method type and limited details such as brand and last four digits,
payout readiness, bounty amounts, private maximums, public bid activity,
charges, fees, refunds, disputes, transfers, settlement status, transaction
history, and accounting or audit records. Payment and payout processing is
provided by Stripe or another provider identified in the Service.

### Marketplace, card, shipping, and verification information

We may collect wishlist entries, card searches submitted to the Service,
bounties, ownership claims, card and packaging photographs, serial or grading
information, claim notes, Verification Partner selection, shipping address,
carrier and tracking information, inspection records, chain-of-custody events,
delivery status, disputes, and communications necessary to complete or protect
a transaction.

### Verification Partner information

If you apply to become a Verification Partner, we may collect shop and owner
names, business and contact information, physical address, operating history,
insurance and security information, inspection capabilities, application
answers, tax and payout information, and program consents.

### Support, safety, and user-provided content

We collect information you send to support, reports of suspected fraud or
prohibited conduct, correction requests, photographs and other evidence, and
any other information you choose to submit. A handle, display name, profile
image, rating, public bounty amount, and other information clearly identified
as public may be visible to other users. Private maximums, payment details,
identity evidence, claim evidence, and private review records are not intended
to be public.

### Device and technical information

Our systems and service providers may process IP address, app and operating
system version, request identifiers, timestamps, authentication and security
events, and server or diagnostic logs. We do not use advertising identifiers
and do not track users across other companies’ apps or websites for
advertising.

### Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses your device location to sort
nearby Verification Partners. In the current version, this location comparison
is performed on your device and Card Bounties does not upload or retain your
precise device location. You can deny or revoke location permission and still
search for Verification Partners manually.

## 2. Where information comes from

We receive information directly from you; automatically from your use of the
Service; from Apple, Stripe, Supabase, carriers, Verification Partners, and
other service providers; from transaction counterparties; and from public or
lawful sources used to operate the card catalog, prevent fraud, or verify a
claim.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts;
- provide card discovery, wishlists, bounties, claims, payments, payouts,
  shipping, inspection, settlement, and support;
- determine account and transaction eligibility and prevent duplicate,
  fraudulent, sanctioned, unsafe, or unauthorized activity;
- calculate and record fees, maintain transaction and accounting records, and
  meet tax, legal, payment-provider, and audit obligations;
- communicate account, bounty, claim, payment, shipping, security, and policy
  updates;
- review Verification Partner applications and administer the program;
- diagnose errors, maintain availability, improve the Service, and protect
  users, cards, our providers, and the public; and
- enforce our Terms of Service and other policies or respond to lawful requests.

We do not use identity documents, payment credentials, private maximums, or
claim evidence for advertising.

## 4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

- **Service providers.** Supabase provides database, authentication, storage,
  realtime, and server-function infrastructure. Stripe provides payment,
  payout, Connect, and Identity services. Apple provides app distribution and
  optional Sign in with Apple. We may also use carriers, insurers, security,
  communications, customer-support, accounting, and professional-service
  providers.
- **Transaction participants.** A buyer, seller, selected Verification Partner,
  carrier, or insurer may receive the information needed to verify, ship,
  deliver, insure, settle, refund, or resolve the applicable transaction. We do
  not disclose another bidder’s private maximum.
- **Legal and safety recipients.** We may disclose information when reasonably
  necessary to comply with law, legal process, payment-network rules, or a
  lawful request; investigate fraud, theft, counterfeit goods, abuse, or a
  security incident; protect rights, safety, users, or property; or establish,
  exercise, or defend legal claims.
- **Business transfers.** Information may be transferred as part of a merger,
  financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of
  the Service, subject to applicable law and appropriate protection.
- **At your direction.** We may disclose information when you ask us to or
  provide informed consent.

We require service providers that process personal information for us to
protect it and use it only for the contracted purpose.

## 5. No sale, targeted advertising, or cross-app tracking

Card Bounties does not sell personal information for money, share personal
information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal
information for targeted advertising. The app does not request permission to
track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose
for which it was collected and for legitimate legal, security, fraud,
accounting, tax, payment-network, dispute, and enforcement needs.

- Account and profile information is generally retained while the account is
  active. When an account is deleted, we delete or de-identify information that
  is no longer needed.
- If an account has an open transaction or binding bounty, deletion may remain
  pending until that obligation is completed, cancelled, or otherwise
  resolved. We will keep the user informed of that status.
- Transaction, ledger, consent, tax, refund, chargeback, shipping, inspection,
  and fraud-prevention records may be retained after account deletion when
  reasonably required by law, providers, accounting standards, dispute
  periods, or the safety of the marketplace. Where practical, retained records
  are separated from the deleted account or de-identified.
- Claim, inspection, and support evidence is retained through the relevant
  verification, delivery, dispute, chargeback, legal-hold, and safety periods,
  then deleted or de-identified when it is no longer needed.
- Stripe controls retention of information collected in Stripe-hosted payment,
  payout, and identity-verification flows under Stripe’s policies and legal
  obligations.
- Deletion from encrypted backups and provider logs may occur on the applicable
  backup or log-rotation schedule.

## 7. Security

We use measures designed to protect personal information, including encrypted
transport, server-side secrets, access controls, row-level database security,
private evidence storage, least-privilege permissions, provider-hosted payment
and identity collection, and auditable transaction operations. No system is
completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Contact us
immediately at **support@northlandai.com** if you believe an account or
transaction has been compromised.

## 8. Your choices and privacy rights

You may edit certain profile information in the app, choose whether to grant
photo or location permission, and revoke optional iOS permissions in Settings.
You can initiate deletion of your Card Bounties account from **Profile →
Settings → Privacy & Security**.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to,
correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of personal information; to
withdraw consent; and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising, or certain
profiling. Card Bounties does not currently sell personal information or use it
for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email **support@northlandai.com** with the subject “Privacy
Request.” We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting. We
will respond within the period required by applicable law. If we deny a request
and your law provides an appeal right, reply with the subject “Privacy Appeal”
and explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. You may also
contact your state attorney general or other applicable regulator.

## 9. Children and age-restricted features

The app's public, read-only information may be browsed without an account, but
the Service is not directed to children under 13. Accounts and all
account-based or transactional features are limited to people who are at least
18 years old. A person under 18 may not create an account, submit personal
information or content, place or claim a bounty, buy or sell a card, or receive
a payout through the Service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information through an account from a
person under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account or provided
personal information, contact us so we can investigate, restrict the account,
and delete the information as appropriate.

## 10. Processing in the United States

Card Bounties is operated from the United States. Information may be processed
in the United States and other locations where our service providers operate,
subject to applicable legal safeguards.

## 11. Third-party sites and services

The Service may link to news sources, manufacturers, grading companies,
carriers, shops, or other third parties. Their privacy practices are governed
by their own policies. Card Bounties is not responsible for a third party’s
independent practices.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the Service, law, or our data
practices. We will post the updated Policy, change the “Last updated” date, and
provide additional notice or obtain consent when required. A material change
will not retroactively authorize a materially different use of previously
collected information without any consent required by law.
